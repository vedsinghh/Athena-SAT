Question
The line graph shows the number of graduates from the classes of 2001 through 2007 at a cer tain school who enrolled in college within 24
months of graduation. Of the following, which class had the fewest graduates who enrolled in college within 24 months of graduation?
Answer
A. 2002
B. 2004
C. 2005
D. 2007
Correct Answer: A
Rationale
Choice A is correct. The year with the fewest graduates who enrolled in college within 24 months of graduation is the point with the lowest value
on the ver tical axis. This occurs at 2002.
Choice B, C, and D are incorrect. The years 2004, 2005, and 2007 each had a greater number of graduates who enrolled in college within 24
months of graduation than did the year 2002.
