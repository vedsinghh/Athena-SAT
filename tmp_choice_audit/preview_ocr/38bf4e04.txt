Question

A factory makes 9-inch, 7-inch, and 4-inch concrete screws. During a certain day, the number of 9-inch concrete screws that the factory makes is
5 times the number 7 of 7-inch concrete screws, and the number of 4-inch concrete screws is 22. During this day, the factory makes 100
concrete screws total. Which equation represents this situation?

Answer

A. 9(5n) + 7n + 4(22) = 100

B.9n+ 7n + 4n = 100

C.5n+ 22 = 100

D. 6n+ 22 = 100
