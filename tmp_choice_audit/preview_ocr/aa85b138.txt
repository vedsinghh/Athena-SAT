Question

2n+6=14

A tree had a height of 6 feet when it was planted. The equation above can be used to find how many years n it took the tree to reach a height of
14 feet. Which of the following is the best interpretation of the number 2 in this context?

Answer

A. The number of years it took the tree to double its height

B. The average number of feet that the tree grew per year

C. The height, in feet, of the tree when the tree was 1 year old

D. The average number of years it takes similar trees to grow 14 feet
