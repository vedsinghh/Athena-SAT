Question
d= 16t
The given equation represents the distance d, in inches, where t represents the number of seconds since an object started moving. Which of the
following is the best interpretation of 16 in this context?
Answer
A. The object moved a total of 16 inches.
B. The object moved a total of 16¢ inches.
C. The object is moving at a rate of 16 inches per second.
D. The object is moving at a rate of + inches per second.
