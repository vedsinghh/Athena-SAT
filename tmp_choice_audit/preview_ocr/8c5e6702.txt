Question

A window repair specialist charges $220 for the first two hours of repair plus an hourly fee for each additional hour. The total cost for 5 hours of
repair is $400. Which function f gives the total cost, in dollars, for 2 hours of repair, where x > 2?

Answer

A. f(x) = 60x + 100

B. f(x) = 60x + 220

Cc. f(z) = 80x

D. f(x) = 80x + 220
