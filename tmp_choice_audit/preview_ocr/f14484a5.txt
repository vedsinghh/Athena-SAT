Question

A manufacturing plant makes 10-inch, 9-inch, and 7-inch frying pans. During a certain day, the number of 10-inch frying pans that the
manufacturing plant makes is 4 times the number 7 of 9-inch frying pans it makes, and the number of 7-inch frying pans it makes is 10. During
this day, the manufacturing plant makes 100 frying pans total. Which equation represents this situation?

Answer

A. 10(4n) + 9n + 7(10) = 100

B. 10n + 9n+ 7n = 100

C.4n+ 10 = 100

D.5n+10= 100
